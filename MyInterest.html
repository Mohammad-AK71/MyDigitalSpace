My Interests

1. Exploring New Things

I'm naturally curious, and I love discovering things I don't know about yet. Whether it's learning something new, exploring an interesting topic, or figuring out how something works, I'm always ready to explore. For me, every new discovery is an opportunity to learn something exciting and see the world from a different perspective. My motto: Stay curious, keep exploring! 🚀

2. Reading Books

Books are like little doors to new worlds. They let me discover new ideas, learn interesting facts, and look at things from different perspectives — all without leaving my chair! I enjoy reading because it helps me expand my knowledge, improve my thinking, and keep my imagination alive. There's always something new to discover on the next page. Fun thought: Every book has something new to teach me! 📖

3. Drawing & Creativity

Drawing is one of my favourite ways to express my creativity. I enjoy turning the ideas in my head into something visual, experimenting with shapes, and bringing imagination to life on paper. Sometimes, a simple sketch can become a whole new idea! I also enjoy learning the basics of graphic design because it helps me understand how colours, layouts, and visuals work together. My creative rule: Imagine it, sketch it, make it real! 🖌️
